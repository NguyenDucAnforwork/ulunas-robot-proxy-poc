## 2. Procedure (unchanged from the brief, 8 steps)

1. Confirm the failure is reproducible.
2. Check whether the input falls inside the supported operating envelope (`INPUT_SPEC.md`) — if not, the "failure" may simply be out-of-envelope use, not a model defect.
3. Bucket failures by robot_state / motor_fan_servo_state / surface / distance / angle / environment / SNR / device.
4. Identify the top 3 factors most strongly associated with failure.
5. Run counterfactual/matched-pair tests: hold speech constant vary noise; hold noise constant vary speaking style; hold content constant vary microphone/device; hold microphone constant vary distance/angle.
6. Report effect size and confidence interval for each counterfactual.
7. Explicitly separate: correlation vs. matched-pair evidence vs. undetermined.
8. **If metadata is insufficient, the conclusion is "insufficient data to determine" — never a guess.**

## 4. Evaluation-based failure analysis (real data, `F_PROXY_ROBOT_ASR` on the robot-proxy synthetic test set, 563 utterances)

Source files: `qc_analysis/{worst_10_cases,best_10_cases,sig_bak_tradeoff_top10}.json`, `qc_analysis/case_audio/*` (actual noisy/clean/enhanced WAVs + spectrograms for every case), `qc_analysis/sig_bak_scatter.png`, `qc_analysis/breakdown_summary.json`. Generated by `qc_analysis.py` + `qc_export_audio.py`, run against the real `evaluation_robot_proxy_results.json`.

### 4.1 Breakdown by robot state, SNR, noise source (real numbers, from `evaluation_robot_proxy_results.json::breakdown`)

For `F_PROXY_ROBOT_ASR`:

| Axis | Best WER | Worst WER |
|---|---|---|
| By SNR bin | 5.3% (10-15dB) | 16.3% (-5-0dB) — monotonic, as expected |
| By noise source | 6.5% (generic) | 12.7% (UAV) — UAV ego-noise is the hardest source by a wide margin |
| By robot state | 4.6% (`rpm_transition`) | 24.7% (`fan`) — broadband fan noise is the hardest single state |

**`idle` is unexpectedly hard** (17.4% WER for `F_PROXY_ROBOT_ASR`, worse than `motor_high` at 12.0%), despite being the quietest procedural state by design. Hypothesis: `idle`'s deliberately low noise floor means the random SNR draw effectively produces very quiet *and* very loud mixtures with little middle ground relative to its own noise level, plus the small sample size (n=12) makes this estimate noisy — **this is a correlation observed in the data, not yet confirmed by a matched-pair counterfactual test** (no controlled `idle`-vs-other-state-at-matched-SNR comparison was run). Flagged as an open question, not a conclusion.

### 4.2 SIG-BAK tradeoff investigation (real evidence)

The hypothesis under test: does the model over-suppress, raising BAK (less residual noise) while lowering SIG (damaged speech)? **Yes, and the evaluation data shows exactly where**: the top-10 cases ranked by (ΔBAK − ΔSIG) vs. noisy input are dominated by **UAV `motor_high`** (7 of 10 cases) and **procedural `fan`** (1 of 10), with ΔBAK vs noisy of +1.8 to +2.5 and ΔSIG vs noisy of **-0.34 to -0.97** (SIG actually *drops below the noisy baseline* while BAK rises sharply). This is a real, checkpoint-verified instance of the SIG-BAK tradeoff, concentrated specifically in the two hardest noise types identified in §4.1 (UAV motor harmonics, fan broadband) — not spread evenly across all conditions. See `qc_analysis/sig_bak_scatter.png` for the full scatter across all 6 conditions.

**Root-cause reasoning (not yet a matched-pair-verified causal claim — see caveat below)**: motor-harmonic noise (UAV) has strong low-frequency tonal components that spectrally overlap with voiced-speech harmonics (pitch + formants), which is exactly the condition under which a masking-based SE model is most likely to attenuate real speech harmonics along with the noise harmonics it resembles — consistent with the "harmonic noise mistaken for voiced speech" and "speech harmonics deleted" hypotheses named in the brief. Fan noise is broadband and directly overlaps the full speech spectrum, so aggressive suppression there also risks removing speech energy, particularly weaker consonants. **This reasoning is plausible and consistent with the acoustic characteristics of the noise types involved, but no counterfactual (e.g., re-synthesizing the same speech content with only the noise type swapped, holding SNR/level fixed) was run to isolate noise-type causation from other confounds (e.g., these are also disproportionately low/negative-SNR items) — so this is reported as a well-supported correlation with a plausible mechanism, not a confirmed matched-pair causal finding.**

### 4.3 Worst-10 / Best-10 cases (real, from the actual test set)

- **Worst 10** (by per-utterance WER, `F_PROXY_ROBOT_ASR`): dominated by low/negative-SNR `generic` and `uav motor_high` cases (WER 0.75-1.0). One outlier (idx 158) has WER=1.0 at a relatively favorable SNR of +14.4dB — inspecting its metadata, this is very likely a short-reference-utterance variance artifact (WER on a 2-3 word reference is highly discretized: one ASR error can mean 33-100% WER) rather than a genuine enhancement failure; flagged as a measurement-granularity caveat, not investigated further with an assumed cause.
- **Best 10**: WER=0.0 for all 10, all `generic_background` noise, spanning SNR from -1.7dB to +8.2dB — showing the model can achieve perfect transcription even at low SNR when the noise is the (in-domain, well-represented) generic type, reinforcing that noise *type*, not just SNR, drives outcomes.
- Actual audio (noisy/clean/enhanced) and spectrograms for every one of these 30 cases are saved under `qc_analysis/case_audio/` for manual listening/inspection.

### 4.4 What was NOT done (honest gaps)

- No counterfactual/matched-pair tests (brief §6's "giữ speech, thay noise" etc.) were run — the analysis above is drawn from the natural variation in the random test set, not controlled swaps. This is a real limitation: the SIG-BAK tradeoff finding in §4.2 is evidence-based correlation with a plausible mechanism, explicitly not elevated to "proven causation."
- `harmonic noise mistaken for voiced speech` and `consonant deletion` were reasoned about qualitatively from spectrograms and noise-type physics, not measured directly (e.g., no formant-tracking or phoneme-level deletion analysis was performed). Anyone reviewing `qc_analysis/case_audio/*_spec_*.png` can verify the qualitative pattern (visible harmonic-band gaps in the enhanced spectrograms of the worst UAV/fan cases vs. their noisy counterparts) but this was not quantified.
- This is evaluation-based analysis on synthetic mixtures, not real-world deployment QC — §1-3's BLOCKED status for actual QC reports is unaffected by this section.
