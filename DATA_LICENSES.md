## Net effect on downstream use

Because `F_PROXY_ROBOT` and `F_PROXY_ROBOT_ASR` include the KU Leuven UAV data at 35% mixture weight, **both checkpoints inherit a non-commercial restriction** from CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0. `F_GENERIC` and `F_GENERIC_ASR` (M1, no UAV data) are not subject to this restriction from data (they still carry whatever restriction the base `model_trained_on_dns3.tar` pretrained checkpoint itself may carry — see `SPEC_PLAN.md` for that checkpoint's own MIT-licensed repo origin).

If a commercial product ever needs a robot-proxy-adapted model, the UAV noise component would need to be replaced with a commercially-licensed or fully-procedural noise source, and the model retrained.
